# Balatro Duel & Co-op

Play Balatro with your Steam friends: team up in **Co-op** or go head to head in a **Duel**.

## Two modes

- **Co-op (2–4 players):** everyone plays their own run on the same seed, but your scores are
  pooled into one shared blind. Blind requirements scale with the number of players, and if you
  run out of hands, your teammates can still carry you through. Beat Ante 8 together.
- **Duel (1v1):** you and your opponent play the same seed side by side. Each blind awards a point
  for the best single hand and a point for beating it in the fewest hands. Outlast your opponent,
  or outscore them if you both make it to the end.

You play at your own pace within each round, and the game keeps everyone in sync between rounds.
Press **Tab** at any time to look at the other players' jokers, deck and hand levels.

## How to play

- Works on **Windows and Mac**, and Windows and Mac players can play together.
- Multiplayer goes through **Steam friend invites**. There's no server to set up and no port
  forwarding.
- Everyone needs their own copy of Balatro (on Steam) and this mod.
- Click **MULTIPLAYER** on the main menu, host a lobby, and invite your friends.

Requires [Steamodded](https://github.com/Steamodded/smods) and
[Lovely](https://github.com/ethangreen-dev/lovely-injector).
