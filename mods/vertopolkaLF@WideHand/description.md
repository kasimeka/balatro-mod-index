# Wide Hand

A Balatro mod for when your hand, jokers, or consumables are so crowded that the cards overlap into an unreadable pile.

Hover a crowded area to spread its cards across more of the screen, separate cards around the cursor like a magnifier, or do both. Move the cursor away and the layout returns to normal. Dragging cards to reorder them works while the area is widened.

The mod only activates when cards are packed closer than half a card width. Settings let you choose the effect and which areas it applies to. Mouse only; disabled while using a gamepad.

Requires Steamodded 1.0.0 beta or newer and Lovely. See the [README](https://github.com/vertopolkaLF/balatro-wide-hand-mod#readme) for installation details and screenshots. Source and releases: [GitHub](https://github.com/vertopolkaLF/balatro-wide-hand-mod).
